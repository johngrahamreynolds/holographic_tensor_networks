# Spin Foams

## Overview

Spin foams are 2-complexes (2-dimensional combinatorial structures) that provide histories or transitions between spin networks. They play the role of Feynman paths in loop quantum gravity, with spin foam amplitudes giving transition probabilities between different spin network states.

A spin foam consists of:
- **Faces**: Labeled by group representations (spins)
- **Edges**: Labeled by intertwiners
- **Vertices**: Labeled by vertex amplitudes

Spin foams implement the dynamics of loop quantum gravity, providing a sum-over-histories formulation similar to path integrals in quantum field theory.

## Tensor Network Interpretation

Spin foams can be viewed as:
- Higher-dimensional tensor networks (2-complexes rather than graphs)
- Tensor network renormalization flows between different scales
- Connections to holographic tensor networks through the bulk/boundary correspondence
- Structures that encode both spatial geometry (spin networks) and spacetime geometry (spin foams)
